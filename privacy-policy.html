Privacy Policy

Dump Wizard · Boca Raton, FL 33428 · Last updated January 2026

Dump Wizard is a family-owned junk removal and dumpster rental company serving Palm Beach and Broward counties. We keep this simple: we only collect what we need to quote and complete your job, and we do not sell your information.

Information we collect

When you call, text, email, or submit a quote request through this website, you may share:

  • Your name and the city where the work is needed
  • Your phone number and email address
  • The service address for a pickup, cleanout, or dumpster delivery
  • A description or photos of the items you want removed

How we use it

  • To give you a flat quote and schedule your service
  • To contact you about arrival windows, delivery times, and pickups
  • To keep a record of completed jobs for our own bookkeeping
  • To answer follow-up questions about work we performed

What we do not do

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We do not send marketing messages to people who have only asked for a quote, and we do not share your details with third parties except where required to complete your job (for example, lawful disposal or donation partners such as Goodwill receive items, never your contact details) or where required by law.

Quote requests submitted on this site

The quote form on this website does not store your information on a server. It opens a message in your own email application addressed to dumpwizardfl@gmail.com, which you send yourself. Your details reach us only when you choose to send that message.

Photos of completed work

We sometimes photograph before-and-after results of a cleanout to show the quality of our work. These photos show the space and the items, never identifying details about you, and we will not publish a photo of your property if you ask us not to.

Data retention and security

We keep job records only as long as we need them for scheduling, bookkeeping, and follow-up. Records are handled by the family members who run the business, and access is limited to them.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to tell you what information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Call or text (561) 980-3444 or email dumpwizardfl@gmail.com and we will take care of it.

Children's privacy

Our services are offered to adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will post the revised version on this page with a new "last updated" date.
