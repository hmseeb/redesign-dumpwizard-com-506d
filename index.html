Frequently asked

The most common questions.

If your question isn't on the list, give us a call — we answer the phone.

How does pricing work?

Every job starts with a free, no-obligation quote. Call or text (561) 980-3444 — a few photos of the items are usually all we need to price the job. What you pay depends on how much space your items take up on our trailer, and the quote we give you is the price you pay: no hidden fees, no surprises.

Do you offer same-day junk removal?

Often, yes. We're open 24 hours a day, seven days a week, and if you call early in the day we can frequently be there the same afternoon. Even when same-day isn't possible, we'll get you the next available window and show up on time.

What areas do you serve?

We serve Palm Beach and Broward counties in South Florida, including Boca Raton, Delray Beach, Boynton Beach, Parkland, Coral Springs, Coconut Creek, Deerfield Beach, Lake Worth, Margate, and the surrounding communities. Not sure if you're in range? Call or text and we'll tell you right away.

What items do you take?

Almost everything: furniture, appliances, mattresses, electronics, yard waste and storm debris, construction debris, hot tubs, tires, and full contents of garages, storage units, estates, and rental properties. Our trucks and trailers haul up to 8 tons per trip, so even big cleanouts usually get done in a single visit.

Is there anything you can't take?

We can't accept hazardous materials or items that require special permits to transport or dispose of. If you're not sure whether something qualifies, just ask when you call — we'll tell you honestly and point you toward the right disposal option if we can't take it.

What happens to my items after you haul them away?

Donation first, landfill last. Usable items — furniture, books, appliances, household goods — are donated to Goodwill, and we recycle what we can. Only what's truly unusable goes to the landfill. It keeps good things in the community and your junk out of the ground.

How much does dumpster rental cost?

Our rental is $350 for up to 48 hours, then $50 per day after that. We also offer optional cancellation insurance for $49, which lets you cancel or reschedule your rental. Once you book, we contact you to schedule your delivery date and time.

How does the dumpster rental process work?

It's simple: book your rental, and we'll reach out to schedule a delivery date and time that works for you. We drop the dumpster where you want it, you fill it at your own pace — up to 48 hours included, with extra days at $50/day — and when you're done, we haul it all away and handle the disposal responsibly.
