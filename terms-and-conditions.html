Items we cannot accept

We cannot accept hazardous materials or items that require special permits to transport or dispose of, including but not limited to solvents, fuels, paints, chemicals, asbestos, and medical waste. If you are unsure whether an item qualifies, ask when you call and we will tell you honestly and point you toward the right disposal option if we cannot take it.

You confirm that you own the items being removed or are authorized to have them removed, and that you can provide safe access to the property. We are licensed and insured, and we take care to protect floors, walls, and doorways. Please point out anything fragile or any keepsake you want flagged before we begin. Pre-existing damage and normal wear associated with removing large or heavy items from tight spaces are not our responsibility.

What happens to your items

Donation first, landfill last. Usable furniture, books, appliances, and household goods are donated to Goodwill, and we recycle what we can. Once items are loaded onto our trailer, ownership transfers to Dump Wizard and they cannot be returned, so please double-check that nothing you want to keep is in the pile.
